Pocket Arcade - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary

Pocket Arcade does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app works entirely offline.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • It contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data stored on your device

Your best score for each game is saved locally on your device so it is still there next time you play. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
